**Date:** 2026-09-19

journeys. Acceptance: quota, missing media/font, catalog outage, stale tab, restore corruption

One flake was observed and resolved: the first full e2e run failed
`presentation-offline.spec.ts:287` (“a builder that cannot be fetched says how to recover and needs
a reload”) at its first `Saved locally` assertion. The spec passed **3/3** on an isolated re-run and
the full suite passed cleanly on re-run, so the failure is recorded as a contention flake, not a
product regression. No other failures were left unexplained.
