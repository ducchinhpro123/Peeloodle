# StickerLab

StickerLab creates editable photo stickers and organizes them into packs.

## Language

**Draft**:
The current editable state of an open sticker project, including changes that may not yet be saved. A draft can belong to a new project or a previously saved project.

**Draft saving**:
Preserving a draft's document and its referenced images and masks locally so the saved composition can be reopened.

## Presentations (planned)

**Presentation**:
An editable, ordered collection of slides intended to communicate a topic to an audience.

**Slide**:
One page of a presentation, containing arranged text, images, and other visual elements.

**Presentation template**:
A reusable starting presentation whose content and design a student can customize for their own presentation.

**Catalog collection**:
An administrator-curated grouping of reusable stickers and icons offered for insertion into presentations. It is distinct from a student's sticker pack.

**Published catalog item**:
An asset or presentation template made available by the administrator for students to use.

**Archived catalog item**:
An asset or presentation template withdrawn from new use in the catalog. Copies already saved in students' presentations remain part of those presentations.

**Presentation backup**:
A portable copy of a presentation and its required artwork that can be restored as an editable presentation in StickerLab. It is distinct from PDF or PPTX output.

**Personal sticker snapshot**:
A copy of a saved sticker's appearance inserted into a presentation as one image. Changes to the source sticker do not change the snapshot.
