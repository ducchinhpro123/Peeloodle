### VERDICT

The document answers the user's question only partly, and its confidence is unevenly earned. Its strongest material — §6 (do not adopt a sync engine) and §9's migration/preflight/measurement trio — survives my checking: I re-derived the persistence claims from `src/lib/persistence/**`, `document.ts`, `store.ts` and `HANDOFF.md`, and they hold, so those sections' advice is sound. But the ranked list is topped by dev tooling (a11y lint, committed snapshots, bundler/knip), the one capability recommendation it leads with is factually void (F1: search already exists and is e2e-covered), and the global framing ("nothing a library can add; the wins are not libraries") is asserted over a search space its own sources explicitly refused to claim ("I could not search npm… the fact pass should try to falsify items 1–5"). As a bounded answer — "in the four scanned dimensions, adopt nothing for the engine; here is the cheapest real work" — it is defensible and worth keeping; as a global answer to "which library could boot this app to another level" it is not yet earned, and a bounded second pass over the untouched dimensions (PWA/offline packaging, server-state and search at catalog scale, admin grid/publish tooling, paste/import sanitization, coverage) is warranted before delivery. Correcting F1, F2, M1's labels and adding the search-space bound to the headline would make the briefs' five executive statements honest; the ratings above do not require any restructuring of §6–§9.
